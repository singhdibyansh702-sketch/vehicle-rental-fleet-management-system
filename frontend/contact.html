Frequently Asked Questions

Is a security deposit required?

No! ApexDrive charges zero security deposit for verified drivers holding an Indian or International driving license.

What documents do I need to bring?

You only need a valid original Government-issued Driving License and an Aadhaar / Passport for identity verification during key handover.

What is the fuel policy?

We operate on a full-to-full basis. You receive the vehicle with a 100% full tank and return it full, avoiding any markup fuel charges.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to pickup time receive a 100% full refund directly credited back to your account.
