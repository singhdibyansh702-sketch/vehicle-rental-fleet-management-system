Redefining Self-Drive & Fleet Operations

ApexDrive is a modern, reliable Vehicle Rental & Fleet Management platform designed for straightforward self-drive bookings, transparent pricing, and robust fleet dispatch.

Our Mission

To make personal and family road trips simple, safe, and transparent. We eliminate hidden surcharges, offer upfront pricing, and ensure every vehicle is thoroughly inspected and cleaned prior to dispatch.

Whether you need a fuel-efficient hatchback for quick city commutes, an executive sedan for business travel, or a rugged 7-seater SUV for family getaways, ApexDrive guarantees prime road readiness.

Full-Stack Technical Architecture

  • Backend Framework: Spring Boot 3.5 on Java 25 LTS
  • Persistence & ORM: Spring Data JPA & Hibernate 6
  • Database Engine: MySQL 8.x / MariaDB with H2 fallback
  • Security & Auth: Spring Security with JJWT tokens
  • Frontend UI: Responsive semantic HTML5, CSS3 & Modern ES6
