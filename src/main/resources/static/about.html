Engineering Excellence

Redefining Self-Drive & Fleet Operations

ApexDrive is a full-stack, enterprise-grade Vehicle Rental & Fleet Management platform designed for seamless booking, intelligent fleet maintenance scheduling, and real-time dispatch tracking.

Our Mission

To democratize access to safe, reliable, and premium personal transportation through transparent pricing, zero deposit friction, and state-of-the-art vehicle telematics.

Whether you need a fuel-efficient hatchback for quick urban errands, a rugged 4x4 SUV for highway road trips, or an executive sedan for corporate travel, ApexDrive guarantees ready-to-drive condition.

Full-Stack Technical Architecture

  • Backend Framework: Spring Boot 3.2.5 on Java 17 LTS
  • Persistence & ORM: Spring Data JPA & Hibernate 6
  • Database Engine: MySQL 8.x / MariaDB with H2 In-Memory Fallback
  • Security & Auth: Spring Security 6 with JJWT (HMAC-SHA256)
  • Frontend Architecture: Vanilla Modern ES6+ & Custom Responsive CSS3
